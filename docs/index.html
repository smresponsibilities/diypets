Make your own desktop pet — in pure Rust.

A zero-runtime desktop mascot engine with Multi-Pet Herd Mode (up to 8 pets with independent sizes & speeds), Play Mode physics, Fullscreen Auto-Hide, and a built-in 2D/SVG Pet Studio.

Plug Claude Desktop, Cursor, or Claude Code straight into diypets mcp to celebrate builds, drop bouncy balls, or spawn pets live on your desktop.
